BOLDGenotyper Migration Checklist

Refactoring BOLDGenotyper into a COI-Focused, Haplotype-Centric Genotyping Pipeline

Version: Draft 1.0
Scope: Migration plan for revising BOLDGenotyper into a robust, biologically accurate, reproducible framework for analyzing COI sequences from BOLD.
Audience: Developer implementing the refactor.
Goal: Update pipeline logic, modules, CLI, and documentation to align with current best practices in COI barcoding, species delimitation, and phylogenetics.

⸻

1. Guiding Principles of the Refactor

Core Biological & Methodological Shifts
	•	Replace “genotype” logic with haplotype (ESV) logic.
	•	Based on evidence that exact sequence variants (ESVs) preserve more biologically informative variation than traditional OTUs while maintaining ecological interpretability.
	•	Reference: Porter & Hajibabaei 2020; various COI ESV benchmarking studies.
	•	Address varying BOLD COI sequence lengths using a biologically grounded core shared region + masking approach.
	•	Reference: Min & Hickey 2007; mini-barcode literature showing ~150–250 bp retains substantial discriminatory power.
	•	Incorporate species delimitation tools (ABGD, ASAP, GMYC/PTP) to cluster haplotypes into higher-level molecular operational units (MOTUs/species hypotheses).
	•	Reference: Puillandre et al. 2012, 2021; widely adopted in COI barcoding.
	•	Add explicit workflows for outlier detection, outgroup rooting, phylogeny, and QC flagging.
	•	Emphasize that BOLDGenotyper is a transparent, reproducible complement to BOLD’s BIN/RESL system, not a replacement.
	•	Reference: Ratnasingham & Hebert 2013.

Design Scope
	•	Focus exclusively on COI sequences from BOLD for this revision.
	•	Backwards compatibility is not required (project not yet public).
	•	Preserve downstream modules (phylogeography, visualization) for future updates.

⸻

2. New High-Level Pipeline Structure
	1.	Input & Metadata Parsing
	2.	Orientation Normalization + COI Validation
	3.	Dynamic QC Filtering (Raw + Core-region)
	4.	Codon-aware Alignment → Core Shared Region + Masking
	5.	Haplotype (ESV) Discovery
	6.	Distance Matrix + Nearest-Neighbor Metrics
	7.	Rare/Suspect Haplotype & Outlier Detection
	8.	Haplotype Clustering + Species Delimitation (Optional)
	9.	Phylogenetic Tree (FastTree) + Outgroup Rooting
	10.	Reporting (Master TSV, Summary Tables, Alignments, Trees)

7. Literature References Used in This Migration
	•	Porter & Hajibabaei (2020) — ESV vs OTU performance in COI metabarcoding.
	•	Min & Hickey (2007) — Impact of barcode sequence length on phylogenetic accuracy; justification for core-region trimming.
	•	Hajibabaei et al. — Mini-barcode effectiveness (100–250 bp COI).
	•	Puillandre et al. (2012, 2021) — ABGD and ASAP species delimitation methods.
	•	Ratnasingham & Hebert (2013) — RESL/BIN system used by BOLD.
